what to bring & how it works

the basics

  • When: Wednesday evenings, around sunset. Times get confirmed by text.
  • Where: We push off from Newport Shores in Bellevue. You'll get the exact spot when you sign up.
  • Bring: a swimsuit, a towel or two, water, and warm layers for the ride home.
  • Nice to have: sandals, a hat for the sauna, and a snack to share.
  • Plunging: totally optional. The lake is right there.
  • Weather: if the wind is too much we'll call it, and you'll hear from us by text.
