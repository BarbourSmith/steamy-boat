about the boat

what to bring & how it works

Why?

  • The floating sauna is an experiment in getting together intensionally with people we appreciate and new friends.
  • We aspire to interact with Lake Washington more often and in a more direct and playful way, but it can be cold.
  • The boat is autonomously GPS guided and will navigate us on a 1-2 hour loop, time to relax, connect, and for the brave swim.
  • We're still figuring out what works how the boat works and how we use it.

The basics

  • When/Where: We head out at 6:30 every Wednesday evening from the boat ramp at Newport Yacht Basin. (47.574946, -122.189982)
  • Bring: A swimsuit, sandals, a towel, water. There isn't a lot of dry space to store things on the boat so less is more.
  • Plunging: We will stop somewhere out on the lake to swim, the water is cold.
  • Weather: If the wind is too much we'll call it, and you'll hear from us by text.

Getting there

  • Parking Option 1: There is paid parking at the top of the boat ramp Here (link to Google Maps 47.574918, -122.188292)
  • Parking Option 2: There is free parking on the side of the street here (link to Google Maps 47.574676, -122.181790)
